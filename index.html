Robotics contest

Line follower robot

Built a two-wheel line follower with a five-member team for a robotics contest, from a parts kit to a working robot.

  • Hardware: Arduino UNO, L298N dual motor driver, two IR line sensors, two geared DC motors on a 2WD acrylic chassis with a caster wheel, 2 × 18650 battery pack and power switch.
  • How it works: the IR sensors tell the Arduino whether the line is under the left or right side; the code then drives, slows or reverses each motor through the L298N to steer back onto the line.
  • What I learned: Arduino C/C++ programming, PWM speed and direction control, IR sensor calibration, breadboard wiring, power management, hardware debugging and teamwork under contest time limits.
